🗂️ Merge & Organize PDF

Combine PDFs, delete/reorder/duplicate/rotate pages, and insert blank or image pages. Files stay on your device.

📄
Drop PDF files here or click to choose
You can select more than one PDF — they'll be merged in the order you add them.

Tip: click a page to select it (for rotate/duplicate/delete). Drag any page to reorder it. New blank/image pages are inserted right after your last selected page (or at the end) — then just drag them into place.

🔒
Nothing is uploaded. Merging, rotating, deleting and inserting pages happens entirely inside your browser tab.
Built with pdf-lib & pdf.js — open-source, runs locally.
